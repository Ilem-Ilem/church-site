About Us

"We are a community of believers committed to making disciples of Jesus Christ who love God passionately and serve others selflessly."

"For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do." - Ephesians 2:10

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To see transformed lives impacting our community and beyond through the power of the Gospel, creating a thriving, multi-generational church that reflects God's kingdom on earth.

"Where there is no vision, the people perish." - Proverbs 29:18

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ through worship, teaching, fellowship, and service, equipping believers to live out their faith in every area of life.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." - Matthew 28:19

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do as a church community

Biblical Authority

We submit to the Bible as God's inspired Word and our final authority in all matters of faith and practice.

Authentic Worship

We express our love for God through heartfelt worship that honors Him and engages every believer.

Intentional Community

We pursue genuine relationships that foster spiritual growth, accountability, and mutual care.

Generous Service

We use our God-given gifts and resources to serve others inside and outside the church.

Compassionate Outreach

We actively share the love of Christ through both word and deed in our local community and beyond.

Spiritual Growth

We are committed to helping every believer mature in their faith and discover their God-given purpose.

Our Journey

How God has been faithful through the years

2005

Church Founding

Started with 12 people meeting in a living room with a vision to reach the community.

2008

First Building

Purchased our first facility, accommodating our growing congregation of 150 people.

2012

Community Outreach

Launched our first major community service program, feeding 500 families monthly.

2016

Expansion

Moved to our current location with capacity for 800 worshippers across two services.

2023

Believers Academy

Launched our discipleship training program to equip believers for ministry.

Grow in Your Faith

Our Believers Academy is designed to help you build a strong foundation in your Christian walk through systematic teaching, mentorship, and practical application.

Biblical Foundation

Spiritual Disciplines

Develop habits of prayer, worship, and spiritual growth.

Practical Ministry

Discover your gifts and learn to serve effectively.
