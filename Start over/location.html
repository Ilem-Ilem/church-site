Getting Here

Multiple ways to reach our church location

Driving Directions

From Uyo City Center

Take Ikot Ekpene Road for approximately 2km. Turn right after the flyover. We're located on the right side, easily identifiable by our signage.

Approx. 8-12 minutes

From Airport Road

Follow Airport Road towards town. Turn left onto Ikot Ekpene Road and continue for 1.5km. Our building is on the right, near the commercial area.

Approx. 10 minutes from airport

Parking Information

We have secure parking available within our compound, with designated spots for first-time visitors, families with children, and special needs.

Public Transport & Accessibility

Public Transportation

Several transportation options are available near our location:

  • Ikot Ekpene Road buses - Stop nearby
  • Commercial taxis - Available throughout Uyo
  • Ride-sharing - Bolt and Uber available in Uyo

Accessibility

Our facility is designed to accommodate everyone:

  • Wheelchair accessible entrances and pathways
  • Accessible restroom facilities
  • Mother and baby room available

Nearby Landmarks

We're conveniently located near several prominent landmarks including government buildings, shopping areas, and educational institutions.
