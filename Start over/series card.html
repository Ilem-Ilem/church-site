About This Series

In "Stay Woke (No Shaking)", Dr. Flourish Peters delivers a timely message about spiritual alertness and resilience in the face of adversity. This series explores what it truly means to be spiritually "awake" and how to maintain unshakable faith when everything around you seems to be in turmoil.

Through eight powerful messages, you'll discover biblical principles for standing firm, recognizing spiritual deception, and cultivating a faith that cannot be shaken by circumstances. This series will equip you with practical tools to navigate challenging seasons with confidence and hope.
