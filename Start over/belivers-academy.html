Believers Academy

Grow in the Word. Build foundations. Become established.

"But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ." - 2 Peter 3:18

Deepen Your Faith Journey

Believers Academy is our comprehensive discipleship program designed to help you build a strong biblical foundation, understand core Christian doctrines, and develop a vibrant, growing relationship with Jesus Christ.

Through systematic teaching, practical application, and community support, you'll gain the knowledge and tools needed to live out your faith confidently and impact your world for Christ.

"Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free." - John 8:32

Biblical Foundation

Understand Scripture and core doctrines

Spiritual Growth

Develop prayer life and spiritual disciplines

Community

Learn and grow with fellow believers

Practical Ministry

Apply your faith in everyday life

Learning Path

Structured courses designed for spiritual growth at every stage

Foundation School

Level 1 • 6 weeks

Build your spiritual foundation with essential Christian doctrines, salvation assurance, and basic Bible study methods. Perfect for new believers or those wanting to strengthen their core understanding.

Understanding Salvation
Basic Bible Study Methods
Prayer & Spiritual Disciplines

Membership Class

Level 2 • 4 weeks

Discover your place in the body of Christ. Learn about church membership, spiritual gifts, and how to get connected and serve in our church community.

Church History & Governance
Discovering Spiritual Gifts
Church Membership Commitment

Prerequisite: Foundation School or equivalent

Discipleship Level

Level 3 • 8 weeks

Deepen your walk with Christ through advanced biblical teaching, spiritual warfare understanding, and leadership development for effective Christian living.

Advanced Bible Study Methods
Spiritual Warfare & Authority
Developing a Leadership Mindset

Service Training

Level 4 • 12 weeks

Equip yourself for ministry service. Receive specialized training in areas like teaching, counseling, worship, outreach, and other ministry areas based on your gifts and calling.

Ministry Specialization
Practical Ministry Skills
Mentorship & Practical Application

Available after completing Discipleship Level
