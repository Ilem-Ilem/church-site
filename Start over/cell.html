What Are Cell Groups?

Cell Groups are small gatherings of believers who meet regularly in homes or other locations to study God's Word, pray together, and build meaningful relationships.

These intimate settings allow for deeper connections, spiritual growth, and practical support as we journey together in faith.

Build authentic relationships with fellow believers
